RStudio (repo root), in this order:

  STEP 1 - audit, THEN STOP
  source("scripts/p0_audit_course_strings.r", encoding = "UTF-8")

    Prints the distinct `course` strings students actually typed, with counts,
    and writes a crosswalk template. Nothing downstream can be trusted until
    this has been read by a human.

    Then:
      - open  .../outputs/placement_hours/course_crosswalk_TEMPLATE.csv
      - check every row already pre-filled by exact name match
      - fill programme_code for the rest BY HAND
      - leave it BLANK where genuinely unknown. Blank is honest; a guess is not.
        In particular do not guess between the three dental programmes
        (1070 = 231h, 1071 = 375h, 1230 = 747h) unless the survey text
        distinguishes them.
      - save over  reference/course_crosswalk.csv  in the repo, and commit it

    The crosswalk shipped on this branch is PROVISIONAL. Rows noted
    "provisional" are guesses at likely variants and must be confirmed here.

  STEP 2 - descriptives (composition first)
  source("scripts/p1_descriptives.r", encoding = "UTF-8")

    Stops with an error if fewer than 50% of students carry an hours value.
    If it stops, the answer is the match rate, not a model. Go back to step 1.

    Read P1_band_composition_family.csv BEFORE the outcome table. If the high
    band is overwhelmingly nursing, everything downstream is a nursing-versus-
    everyone comparison and the slides have to be framed that way.

  STEP 3 - Arm P1, the headline
  source("scripts/p2_hours_interaction.r", encoding = "UTF-8")

    Prints the within-course real-value variation diagnostic BEFORE any
    estimate. Bands marked THIN do not support a claim, whatever the p-value.

  STEP 4 - Arm P2, exploratory
  source("scripts/p3_hours_family_fe.r", encoding = "UTF-8")

  STEP 5 - slides (reads the saved tables, refits nothing)
  source("scripts/p4_placement_slides.r", encoding = "UTF-8")
